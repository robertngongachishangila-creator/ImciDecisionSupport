# IMCI Decision Support: User Guide

This guide is for community health workers. The same guide is available inside the app:
tap **📖 User Guide** on the home screen.

> This app supports, but does not replace, clinical judgment. Always follow your IMCI
> training and your supervisor's guidance.

## 1. Opening the app

- **First time:** create a 4-digit PIN and enter it twice.
- **Every time after:** enter your PIN to unlock.
- **Forgot your PIN?** Tap *Forgot PIN?* and then *Reset*. This **deletes all saved
  assessments**, so patient data stays private, and you then create a new PIN.

## 2. Doing an assessment

Tap **🩺 New Assessment**. Answer each step and tap **Next →**. Tap **← Back** at any time
to change an answer.

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 👶 1. Patient info | Enter the child's age in years and months. The app is for children aged 2 months up to 5 years; for a younger or older child, follow the national guidelines. For the ID, use a code (e.g. P123), not the child's name. |
| ⚠️ 2. Danger signs | Check these first: not able to drink or breastfeed, vomits everything, convulsions, lethargic or unconscious. |
| 🌬️ 3. Cough or difficult breathing | Count breaths for one full minute while the child is calm. Look for chest indrawing and listen for stridor. |
| 💧 4. Diarrhoea | Ask how many days and whether there is blood in the stool. Look and feel for dehydration: restless, sunken eyes, how the child drinks, skin pinch. |
| 🌡️ 5. Fever | Ask how many days. Record the malaria test (RDT) result if one was done. Check for stiff neck and measles. |
| 👂 6. Ear problem | Ask about ear pain. Look for pus draining from the ear and ask for how many days. Feel for tender swelling behind the ear. |
| 📏 7. Malnutrition and anaemia | Look for swelling (oedema) of both feet. Measure MUAC (children 6 months and older). If there is oedema of both feet or MUAC is below 11.5 cm, record the result of the RUTF appetite test. Look at the palms for pallor. |

## 3. Reading the results

The child gets **one result for every illness found**. The most urgent results are shown first.

| Colour | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🚑 Pink | Urgent referral | Refer the child to hospital urgently. Give any pre-referral treatment shown. |
| 💊 Yellow | Treat | Give the specific treatment shown and advise the mother. |
| 🏠 Green | Home care | Advise the mother on home care and when to return. |

If any result is pink, the screen also says **"This child needs URGENT REFERRAL"**.

## 4. Saving and history

- Tap **Save Assessment** on the results screen. The button changes to **✅ Saved**.
- Saved assessments stay **only on this phone** and work **without internet**.
- Tap **📋 View History** on the home screen to see past assessments. Tap one to see its
  results and the signs that were recorded, or tap **🗑️ Delete** to remove it.
